Last updated: August 29, 2026.

Math GeoHub Architecture is a static, skills-only plugin. The published package contains written instructions and reference documents only. It includes no executable scripts, MCP server, analytics, telemetry, account system, credential handling, cookies, or network integration.

The plugin itself does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share personal data. Your chosen AI client may process prompts, files, and generated output under that client's own privacy policy and settings; this plugin does not change those controls.

If a future release adds a service, executable component, authentication, or telemetry, this policy and the plugin version will be updated before release.

Questions may be filed through the [project issue tracker](https://github.com/Mola-maker/math-geohub-architecture/issues).
